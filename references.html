References

[1] Ding et al. (2024)

W. Ding, M. Abdel-Basset, I. Alrashdi, and H. Hawash, “Next generation of computer vision for plant disease monitoring in precision agriculture: A contemporary survey, taxonomy, experiments, and future direction,” Information Sciences, vol. 665, 120338, 2024.

Synopsis: This paper surveys modern computer vision techniques for plant disease monitoring, including CNNs, vision transformers, and other recent deep learning architectures. It also discusses challenges such as limited data, computational efficiency, explainability, and real-world deployment.

Why this source is useful: This source supports the overall background of this tutorial and provides a recent overview of computer vision methods used in plant disease monitoring.

View Source

[2] Iftikhar et al. (2024)

M. Iftikhar, I. A. Kandhro, N. Kausar, et al., “Plant disease management: a fine-tuned enhanced CNN approach with mobile app integration for early detection and classification,” Artificial Intelligence Review, vol. 57, article 167, 2024.

Synopsis: This study proposes an enhanced CNN-based system for detecting plant diseases and integrates the model with a mobile application. The work focuses on diseases affecting crops such as apple, corn, and potato.

Why this source is useful: This source directly supports the mobile deployment and CNN classification sections of this tutorial.

View Source

[3] Javidan et al. (2024)

S. M. Javidan, A. Banakar, K. Rahnama, K. Asefpour Vakilian, and Y. Ampatzidis, “Feature engineering to identify plant diseases using image processing and artificial intelligence: A comprehensive review,” Smart Agricultural Technology, vol. 8, 100480, 2024.

Synopsis: This review examines visual features used for plant disease identification, including color, texture, and shape. It also discusses image processing, feature extraction, and disease-area segmentation techniques.

Why this source is useful: This source supports the preprocessing, visual feature analysis, segmentation, and disease-area measurement sections of the tutorial.

View Source

[4] Leite et al. (2024)

D. Leite, A. Brito, and G. Faccioli, “Advancements and outlooks in utilizing Convolutional Neural Networks for plant disease severity assessment: A comprehensive review,” Smart Agricultural Technology, vol. 9, 100573, 2024.

Synopsis: This review examines how CNN-based methods are used not only to identify plant diseases but also to estimate disease severity. It discusses current methods, limitations, and future research directions.

Why this source is useful: This paper supports the disease severity estimation section and explains why measuring affected leaf area can provide more useful information than classification alone.

View Source

[5] Plant Doctor (2025)

“Plant Doctor: A hybrid machine learning and image segmentation software to quantify plant damage in video footage,” Measurement, 2025.

Synopsis: This study introduces a video-based plant health system using YOLOv8 for leaf detection, DeepSORT for tracking leaves across video frames, and DeepLabV3+ for segmentation and damage quantification.

Why this source is useful: This is one of the most important references for this project because it directly supports the expanded video and multi-view analysis component suggested in the project feedback.
