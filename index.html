Plant Disease Detection Using Mobile Images and Video

Author: Mahitha Rangineni

Introduction

Plant diseases can affect crop health and agricultural productivity. Identifying diseases from leaf appearance can be difficult because symptoms may look similar and real-world images can have different lighting, backgrounds, camera angles, and image quality.

This tutorial explores how computer vision can be used to detect plant diseases from mobile images and video. It covers image preprocessing, classification, object detection, segmentation, and video-based analysis.
