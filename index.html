Project Overview

Plant diseases can significantly reduce crop yields and hurt agricultural productivity. While inspecting plants visually is important for early diagnosis, having experts check every field manually is time-consuming and expensive.

Recent advances in computer vision make it possible to detect plant diseases using smartphone cameras. However, moving from clean laboratory photos to outdoor fields brings real challenges: changing sunlight, cluttered backgrounds with soil and weeds, overlapping leaves, and motion blur [1].

Tutorial Goal

This tutorial explores how computer vision can go beyond basic single-image classification. We will look at mobile data capture, CNN classification, YOLO object detection, pixel-level segmentation for calculating disease severity, DeepSORT video tracking, and practical mobile deployment trade-offs.

Why Use Video and Multi-View Analysis?

Most plant disease projects rely on a single photo of a leaf. In real-world conditions, that single image can easily fail:

  • Overcoming glare and shadows: Bright sunlight or shadows can hide disease spots or make healthy leaves look sick. Video captures multiple angles, helping find clearer views.
  • Combining predictions across frames: Using techniques like majority voting across multiple video frames helps filter out occasional blurry or incorrect predictions.
  • Tracking individual leaves: Tracking algorithms like DeepSORT can follow a specific leaf as the camera moves, preventing the system from counting the same leaf multiple times [5].

What You Will Learn

In this tutorial, you will explore:

  • Mobile Capture & Preprocessing: How lighting, blur, and color adjustments affect model inputs [2], [3].
  • CNN Classification & Transfer Learning: How convolutional networks identify disease categories and why transfer learning is essential for smaller datasets.
  • YOLO Object Detection: How bounding boxes locate multiple infected leaves in cluttered outdoor scenes [1].
  • Segmentation & Severity Estimation: Using U-Net and DeepLabV3+ to measure the exact percentage of diseased leaf area [4].
  • Video Analysis & Tracking: How DeepSORT tracks leaves over time and how majority voting combines multi-frame predictions.
  • Plant Doctor Case Study: Examining real experimental results from Montagut Marques et al. (2025) and discussing why real-time video processing remains challenging [5].
  • Deployment Trade-Offs: Comparing on-device mobile processing with cloud-based options.
